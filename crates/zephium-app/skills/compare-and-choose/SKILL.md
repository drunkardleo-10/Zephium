# Compare and choose

## Gather
- Products from a named store: one browser part on that store with records for name, price, rating, url and up to three image_url fields. The page is the source of prices and photos; search snippets never stand in for a displayed price.
- Options the person names on different sites: a research part, or one browser part per site when prices and photos are needed.
- Services or tools you know well: search only for current prices and limits.

## Result
- **picks** of the candidates (three to six), each with its own photo or logo, price as shown, up to four facts that differ between them (typed yes/no/partial where they are checks), the why line, and one recommended.
- A **sheet** beside the picks only when there are more than three shared attributes to line up: first column entity with the logo host or image on its row, typed columns (money with currency, rating like 4/5, yes_no), best on the columns where one value wins. No sentences in cells; unknown stays empty.
- The **reply** names the pick and the reason in one sentence, with the price as a figure.
- Never a table and picks that restate each other: the picks carry the look, the sheet carries the lined-up facts.
