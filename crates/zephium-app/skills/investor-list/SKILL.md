# Investor list

## First
- Know the company's space, stage, round size and where it is based. Ask only what is missing, in one ask with options (stage: "Pre-seed", "Seed", "Series A").

## Parts (in one turn)
- **Funds** — research part: funds that led or joined rounds at this stage in this space in the last eighteen months (funding news, the funds' own portfolio pages), each with the fund's host, the partner who led the deal, the deal with its date, and the check size when published.
- **Angels** — research part, for pre-seed and seed: operators and angels who backed similar companies recently, each with their public page, the deal and its date.
- Each part's brief ends with: report in the digest and place nothing. You build every object from the digests, with no searches of your own after them.

## Result
- One **picks** object, facet company, of the best fits (six to ten; an angel is an item too, subtitled as an angel): name, subtitle what they are ("Seed fund, New York"), the fund's logo host or the angel's own photo from their page, facts for check size, stage, the relevant deal ("Led Plane seed, 2025") and the partner, the why line tying them to this company, the url of their page. The strongest fit is recommended.
- A **sheet** only for more than ten investors: Investor (entity with logo host), Type (tag), Stage (tag), Check (text), Relevant deal (text), Partner (text), Link.
- The **reply**: a headline with the count and where to start ("14 fits; start with Frontline and Seedcamp"), text with why the first two fit.
- Only deals from sources, with their dates. An intro or outreach email is a **draft** only when the person asks, and sending goes through the app's Confirm.
