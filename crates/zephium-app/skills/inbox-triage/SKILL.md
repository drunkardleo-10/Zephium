# Inbox triage

## Read
- One browser part, **Gmail**, on mail.google.com (Outlook on outlook.office.com or outlook.live.com when the person uses it). A mail connection the person has is offered by the app and used instead. Brief: the primary inbox's unread mail and mail from the last three days, at most forty messages; for each the sender, subject, time, the ask in their own words and the thread's link; mark newsletters, receipts and notifications as such without opening them.
- The part reads the person's own account; the app asks first. Never put their mail into a search, and never archive, label or send from the part.
- The part's brief ends with: report in the digest and place nothing. You build every object from its digest.

## Result
- One **list**, style messages, titled "Needs you": one item per message that asks something of the person, most urgent first; title says what they want in a few words ("Ana needs the Q3 numbers by Friday"), from with app Gmail, host, who, when, the ask as quote and the thread's link; due when a date is stated; priority high for what blocks someone or is due in two days. At most fifteen; merge a thread's messages into one item.
- A **draft** for each of the most urgent replies that need writing, at most three: destination email, to, subject "Re: …", a short body in plain words that answers what was asked and leaves a clear blank where only the person knows the answer. Sending goes through the app's Confirm.
- The **reply**: a headline with the shape ("3 need replies today, 14 can be archived"), figures for needs reply and can be archived.
- When the part could not read the inbox, its row shows the fix and there is no list.

## Follow-ups
- "Archive the rest": the part archives exactly the messages it marked, after the app's Confirm.
