# Meeting prep

## Read
- **Calendar** — browser part on calendar.google.com (the person's calendar connection when the app offers it): the meeting the request names, else the next one today; its time, place or call link, attendees with their emails, description and attached documents.
- **Mail** — browser part on mail.google.com, and **Slack** on app.slack.com when the person uses it: the last threads with these attendees or on the meeting's subject from the past month; each with who, when, the open question or promise in their own words and the link.
- **People** — research part, only for attendees outside the person's company: their role and company from public pages (the company's site, their own page), and the company's news from the last three months.
- When the request names the attendees or their company, start every part in one turn; otherwise start Calendar first and the rest with the attendees it found. The person's text never goes into a search: only attendees' names and companies do.
- Each part's brief ends with: report in the digest and place nothing. You build every object from the digests, with no searches of your own after them.

## Result
- One **picks** object, facet person, of the outside attendees: name, role and company as subtitle, a photo only from their own public page (else the company's logo host), facts for what matters here (the deal, the last contact), and the url of their page.
- One **list**, style todo, titled "To raise": what to settle or ask, verb-first ("Confirm the renewal price Dana asked about"), each with from pointing at the thread or document it comes from. At most seven.
- The **reply**: a headline with the meeting ("Acme renewal at 14:00 with Dana and Lee"), text naming the one thing to settle, points for context that changes the conversation (the last number discussed, a complaint, the company's news).
- An agenda or follow-up email is a **draft** only when the person asks.
