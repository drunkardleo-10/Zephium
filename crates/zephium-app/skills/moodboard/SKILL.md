# Moodboard

## Gather (in one turn)
- Reference links in the request: one browser part, **References**, that reads each link and returns its images.
- A direction in words: two browser parts with the direction as a short query of two to four words ("warm brutalist editorial"): **Are.na** on are.na (channels and blocks) and **Dribbble** on dribbble.com (shots), or Behance on behance.net for print and branding. Each returns eight to ten works with title, maker, url and up to three image_url fields of the work itself, never the site's banner or an avatar.
- Each part places its works as one picks object on its row (facet other), chosen to agree with each other: a board is one direction, not a search result.

## Result
- The parts' **picks** are the board: each work with its own image, the work's title as name, its maker as subtitle, its url, tags for what it contributes (grain, serif, mono, duotone; at most three), and a why line saying what to take from it; the anchor work, the one the rest follow, recommended. Put this in each part's brief. You make no picks of your own.
- The **reply**: a headline naming the direction in a phrase ("Raw concrete, cream paper, one signal red"), text of one sentence on the mood, and points for Palette, Type, Texture, Composition and Avoid, in words taken from the works (hex values only when a source states them).
- Pictures are the board: no sheet, no list of links, no document.

## Follow-ups
- "Warmer", "less busy", "more like the third": one part's search again with the changed words, then revise the picks in place.
