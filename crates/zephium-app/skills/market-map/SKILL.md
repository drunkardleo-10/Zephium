# Market map

## Parts (in one turn)
- **Players** — research part: the companies in the market from recent sources (analyst maps, funding news, category pages on G2 or Product Hunt), each with its site's host, founding year, total raised and last round with its date, and the one line it sells.
- **Segments** — research part: how the market divides (by customer, by approach, by layer of the stack) from how the companies and analysts describe it, with the companies in each.
- A market the person names narrowly ("AI note-takers for doctors") needs only Players; derive the segments from it.
- Each part's brief ends with: report in the digest and place nothing. You build every object from the digests, with no searches of your own after them.

## Result
- One **diagram** as the map, read left to right: a layer per segment (at most six, named in two or three words), a node per company (at most twenty-four: the leaders and the notable newcomers), kind external, vendor its bare host so its logo shows, note the one fact that places it ("Series B · $62M"). Edges only for real relations between companies (acquired, built on); none is fine.
- One **sheet** of the facts: Company (entity with its logo host), Segment (tag), Founded (number), Raised (money with currency), Last round (tag: Seed, Series A…), Link. Sorted by raised.
- The **reply**: a headline naming the shape of the market ("Six segments; crowded in scribing, open in billing"), text naming the gap worth a company, points for consolidation, pricing pressure or a platform threat when the sources show one.
- Only companies from sources; an unknown figure stays empty.
