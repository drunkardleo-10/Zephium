# Campaign plan

## First
- Know what is launched, for whom, the launch date and the budget. Ask only what is missing, in one ask with options (for a budget: "No paid ads", "Under $2,000", "$2,000–10,000").

## Gather
- One research part, **Channels**, when the audience or the market is not obvious: where this audience gathers (communities, newsletters, subreddits, podcasts, launch sites), with each place's size or reach and its rules for self-promotion, from sources. Skip it when the person named the channels.

## Result
- One **plan** from today to a week after launch: each step dated (when as a date or "Week 2"), title a verb and a channel ("Open the waitlist on the landing page", "Launch on Product Hunt at 00:01 PT"), detail with what to prepare and why, kind milestone for launch day and for moments that gate the rest, task for work, event for fixed dates; cost where money is spent; checkable true. Three to five channels that suit this audience, not every channel there is.
- A **draft** for each launch-day message, at most three, shaped like its destination: an X post (under 280 characters), a LinkedIn post, an email to the list (with subject). They say one thing, the same thing, in the voice of the product. Posting and sending go through the app's Confirm.
- The **reply**: a headline with the shape ("Four weeks to a Tuesday launch, three channels"), text with the core message in one sentence, figures for launch day and the budget.
- No list of generic tips, no step the agent could do right now (it drafts instead).
