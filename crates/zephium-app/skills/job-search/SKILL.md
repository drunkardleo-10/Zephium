# Job search

## First
- Know the role, level, place or remote, and anything that rules a job out. Ask only what is missing, in one ask with options.

## Parts
- **Boards** — browser part on the board or career pages that fit (the company's own careers page, its Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever board, LinkedIn Jobs when the person is signed in): records for title, company, location, salary when shown, url.
- One part per named company when the person lists several; research part for "who is hiring for X".

## Result
- **picks** with facet job: title as name, company and location as subtitle, the company's logo host, salary as price when shown, facts for remote, level and when posted, the why line tying it to the person's ask, the posting's url; one recommended.
- A **sheet** only for more than eight roles: Company (entity with logo host), Role, Location, Salary (money when shown), Remote (yes_no), Link.
- The **reply**: the count, the best match and why in one sentence.
- Applying goes through the page and the app's Confirm; drafts of cover letters are a **draft** only when asked.
