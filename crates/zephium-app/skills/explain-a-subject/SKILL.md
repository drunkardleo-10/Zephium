# Explain a subject

- Explain from your own knowledge; search only for something recent or specific you do not know.
- The **reply** carries the explanation's core: a headline that states the idea in plain words, text of one to three sentences a newcomer can follow, and points (up to five) for the steps of the mechanism in learning order, prerequisites first.
- A **diagram** of the mechanism when it has parts or a flow: nodes for the parts, edges labelled with what passes between them, layers for levels. Typographic nodes (no vendor) unless a node is a real product.
- When the subject belongs to a programming language, library or runtime, one **code** object: a short complete example (at most thirty lines) with a note on each line that carries a concept.
- A **sheet** of terms (Term, Meaning as short text) only when there are more than five terms.
- Never a long document, never findings.
