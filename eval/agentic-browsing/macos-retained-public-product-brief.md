## Frozen authority and independent result checks

The task accepts no runtime URL, selector, script, schema or target argument.
Its sole initial HTTPS document is the listing above; no document continuation
or redirect expansion is admitted. It creates no loopback server and imports no
Browse tab, account or profile. The one owned, ephemeral, extension-free Work
resource and original anonymous account sample keep their existing limits;
task code never renews an account timestamp.

The schema requires `product_name` (at most 64 bytes) and `technical_summary`
(at most 512 bytes). The native predicate requires one unique matching
Heading/accessible-name product identity and one unique Paragraph/visible-text
introduction. The latter is recognized by a frozen short introduction prefix
and chip identifier. Its complete answer is captured from the actual native
source, not supplied in the objective or copied from web research. Missing,
duplicate, wrong-role, foreign or incomplete evidence fails before a model call.
A changed introduction can correctly fail this frozen contract; no automatic
wait, recapture, predicate relaxation or budget increase follows a refusal.

Mapping selects only Heading/Paragraph evidence through the existing trusted
source-role mechanism. The model may inspect the acknowledged initial baseline.
There is no subtree, action, sign-in, subscription, variant/cart/purchase,
navigation or second-observation capability. Both task acceptance and the
application-owned consumer require two exact complete values with one source
per field and the original observation, frame/document/origin, invocation,
snapshot generation, reference, role and source field. Paraphrase, fabrication,
multiple/reordered citations and stale substitution fail. Output is one-shot,
non-durable `ModelMapped` data, not independently established factual truth,
stock/pricing truth or artifact publication. All page/model data stays untrusted.

```sh

  --features macos-work-retained-public-probe \
  --config tauri.work-rendering-probe.conf.json --ci --no-sign
```
